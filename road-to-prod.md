# Historical production roadmap — superseded

**The former “CONFIRMED” production-tier verdicts are withdrawn as current
readiness claims.** They described particular commits and historical scheduled
runs. The scheduled workflows they relied on have since been removed; those old
results cannot establish the health of today's tree or deployment.

[STATUS.md](STATUS.md) is the current readiness statement. See
[compatibility](docs/compatibility.md), [upgrading](docs/upgrade.md) and the
[operator guide](docs/operations.md) before evaluating a deployment.

The former roadmap remains in Git history (for example,
`git show 26abdbaac:road-to-prod.md`). Its audits, counts and closed items are
historical evidence only, not current release gates or a production endorsement.
