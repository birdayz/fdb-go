For `wide_t(id, v)` containing `(2,7)`:

```sql
SELECT w.*, COUNT(*) AS n
FROM wide_t w
GROUP BY 1, 2 AS "W.ID"
```

Expected output is `(2,7,1)`, labelled `ID,V,N`.

Expansion correctly binds `W.ID` and `W.V`. However, [select_parser.go:1634](/home/birdy/projects/fdb-go/pkg/relational/core/embedded/select_parser.go:1634) looks up `ac.groupCol` as an alias without checking whether the output is already bound. [logical_builder.go:181](/home/birdy/projects/fdb-go/pkg/relational/core/embedded/logical_builder.go:181) then trusts that replacement, assigning both visible columns native grouping slot 1. The guard at [logical_predicate.go:4341](/home/birdy/projects/fdb-go/pkg/relational/core/embedded/logical_predicate.go:4341) validates the overwritten identity and cannot catch the corruption.
