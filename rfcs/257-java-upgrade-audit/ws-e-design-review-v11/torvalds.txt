## HIGH

**H1. The guard narrowing admits a hazard the tree pins and documents (design:872-879, :907-908).**
- The design says "The guard exists for one hazard". The tree says otherwise: PIN 1 (`zz_graefe_required_pins_test.go:40-52`) calls the per-alias exception load-bearing. It names the exact danger: "allowing the bb.v residual onto the t-leg merely because the leg has SOME probe-fed correlation (o) … 0 rows". RFC-150 §8 (`rfcs/150-…:204-213`) says the same.
- The prototype's GUARD is `len(probeCorr) > 0` (`fdb-explode-probe/…/planner.go:1482`). The design's own unit case "probe correlated to another alias: admitted" is that case.
- **Measured:** PIN 1 passes under GUARD (`wse12-suites-GUARD.log:19241`).
- **Not measured:** whether PIN 1 reaches the widened branch at all. The `PROBE_CORRLOG` output was not captured.
- **Fix, either:**
  - Name what now carries the per-alias property (RFC-150's task-graph invariant). Prove PIN 1 is not vacuous: capture `CORRSAFE-ADMIT` on its leg, or run a mutation that reddens it. Then rewrite the PIN 1 comment, RFC-150 §8 and the DIVERGENCES entry (`DIVERGENCES.md:3131-3143`).
  - Or narrow the guard only as far as #7 needs: every outer alias of the residual is either fed by the probe or an explode alias of the same select.

**H2. GUARD causes a new 54F02 that the design does not report (design:10-12, :890).**
- Under GUARD, `and-idempotent` reports `compared=59 both-error=1` (`wse12-suites-GUARD.log:23293`); ARM reports `60/0` (`wse12-suites-ARM.log:23325`).
- The failing query is `((id IN (0,1,-2) AND (NOT f AND a IN (-2,0,3)))) AND (same) ORDER BY id`, with `max_task_count=150000, task_count=150000` (`GUARD.log:23226-23228`).
- It stays green only because the sweep's `equiv` error branch logs and returns (`metamorphic_sweep_rewrites_fdb_test.go:85-91`).
- This contradicts "no test" and "no plan regression". The design keeps the guard because it bounds the search budget, and that reason indicts GUARD too.
- **Fix:** root-cause the blow-up (IN explodes × partition arm × admitted compensations). Measure task counts under GUARD against 2, 3, 4 and duplicated IN conjuncts, and pin them. Make the sweep fail on 54F02, or pin its `both-error` counts.

## MEDIUM

**M1. Section 8's NaN declaration is stale (design:3484, :3486).**
- "every later comparison residual" was superseded by 4.1(b)'s binder key filter (:1166).
- "rows are equal for every NaN a statement can write" is false by v11's own round. The division stores `fff8…`/`ffc00000` (`java_t_d_id3`, `java_t_f_id3`), where the CAST stores `7ff8…`. After step (6), the target's probe misses the division row and Go returns it.
- 4.1(b) itself says the rows differ. The grep for the superseded claim was not done: `every NaN a statement` still has one live hit, :3486.

**M2. The census of Go NaN producers is incomplete.**
- `javaMinF64` and `javaMaxF64` (`streaming_cursors.go:2159, 2166`) return `math.NaN()` (`0x7ff8000000000001`). Java's `Math.min`/`Math.max` (`NumericAggregationValue.java:687-693`) return the operand's NaN.
- So `INSERT … SELECT MAX(d)` still writes Go's bits after step (6). That falsifies :1129 ("carries whatever bits its producer gave it in both engines").
- `executor.go:3831` is a third `math.NaN()` site.

**M3. The WS-F dependencies cite a document that is not in the tree.**
