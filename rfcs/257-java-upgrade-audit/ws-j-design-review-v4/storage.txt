## MEDIUM

**2. F13's population is misstated in two directions.**
- **(i) Silent re-framing (SOURCE).** Master's loader resolved the union by name first: `UnionDescriptor`, then `RecordTypeUnion`, then usage=UNION (`e48f5b496:metadata_proto.go:790-812`).
  - A stored file holding `UnionDescriptor` beside a usage=UNION message, or beside a message named `RecordTypeUnion`, now loads under the OTHER union.
  - Its records were framed with `UnionDescriptor`'s field numbers.
  - That is neither refused nor migrated. The migration's (a) arm would itself fail with "Only one union descriptor is allowed".
  - §4c ("What stops loading", :1076) and CHANGELOG omit this shape. Refuse it on load with a specific message, or migrate it.
- **(ii) Arm (b) is not "no union" (:1105).** Master's `SetRecordsWithUnionName(fd, "<custom>")` over an unannotated union framed records by that union. On reload, master fell into union-less mode.
  - "Only the pre-upgrade build reads them" is false, because that build's loader could not read them either.
  - Let the migration annotate a union the caller names, instead of refusing.
