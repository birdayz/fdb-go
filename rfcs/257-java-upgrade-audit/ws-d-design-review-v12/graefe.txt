## P1: D-0's attempt cap does not hold on the tree's third backend
- **What the design claims.** The cap is set by retry limit 0, and the design says "the route is the same on both client backends" (950-953).
- **The third backend.** SimDB (`pkg/simfdb`) opens record-layer databases through `NewFDBDatabaseWithBackend` in 10 non-test places (for example `hunt.go:163`).
  - Its `SetRetryLimit` is accepted and ignored (`options.go:42`).
  - `Transact` retries internally up to 100 times (`simfdb.go:14`, `183-205`).
- **Consequence.** Every DST hunt keeps the old contract: the attempt loop never sees a retryable error, and neither MaxAttempts nor SQL's single attempt applies. The hunts stay green on a contract production no longer has.
- **Fixing the backend alone breaks the hunts.** Honouring the limit invalidates the hunts' assumption that "faults are retried transparently by db.Run, so any surfaced error is a … bug" (`hunt.go:210-212`).
- **Needed:**
  - SimDB honours the retry limit.
  - Hunt error classification is re-derived for each owner's policy.
  - The attempt-count fixture also runs on SimDB.
  - Its 8 caller lines (`hunt.go:258-332`, `continuation.go:368-512`) are classified; no class in 908-942 covers them.
