## P1: B is a work bound, but whether the peel works still depends on time (1466-1492, 1671-1683, 1706-1734)

- **Refusing is better than admitting a slow peel.** A refused peel goes to the reconcile, which clears the task when the count is at or below the hard cap (1466-1469, 1633). An admitted peel that runs past 5 s fails with 1007 on every attempt and leaves the task stuck at the head of its queue (1682).
- **So B needs an upper limit, and v14 has none.** The coverage argument only sets a floor: covering n=1001 at d=2048 needs B ≥ 1.845e7. The value 1.96e7, with its edge at d=980, is left over from the withdrawn time derivation.
- **v14 removed v13's rule for a slower Go** ("B goes down if Go is slower; the fixture moves") and put nothing in its place ("does not move B", 1492).
- **The acceptance fixtures therefore depend on an unmeasured Go rate.** At the highest JVM rate, fixture 2 (W=1.845e7) already spends 3.23 s on refits. Add the target's fit (up to 0.31 s), the floor sort that is not timed, 4 MB of reads and a 5.3 MB commit.
  - "No wall-clock assertion" (1706) is false in effect: the 5 s limit is one.
  - The merger's retries would hide a flaky 1007.
- **The owned 1007 is not priced.** In a merger session it costs about 12 quota halvings × 10 attempts × ~5 s, roughly 10 minutes (IndexingMerger.java:246-258). The target aborts at once on its non-FDB throw (:189-203).
- **Needed:**
  - a stated Go performance criterion at W=B and at every fixture (n≥2 runs, load recorded), where a miss is fixed in Go and never by raising B;
  - fixtures that assert completion in exactly one attempt, through the observer;
  - the price of the 1007, stated.

## P2

1. **The retry predicate follows the wrong Java source (950-958).**
   - `RunRetriable` retries if ANY cause in the chain is a retryable FDBException or a `RecordCoreRetriableTransactionException` (FDBDatabaseRunnerImpl.java:195-207). The design's own 847-851 says the same.
   - `FDBExceptions.isRetriable`, which stops at the first FDB error, is AutoContinuingCursor's predicate (:140).
   - One shared predicate cannot port both. Run needs the any-cause rule.
2. **The EXHAUSTED reconcile hides bugs, and its fixture cannot fail (1044-1061).**
   - Copying store state into the model is the blind resync the v13 storage review warned about. After an exhausted `deleteAll` it resyncs everything.
   - "Model and store equal after the reconcile" holds by construction.
   - Ground truth exists: SimFDB's `LastCommitUnknownApplied` (simfdb.go:56-80), read on the committing goroutine, and the chaos arm knows whether it committed.
   - Calls whose every attempt was 1020 or 1007 are known not-applied, so they are not unknown outcomes.
3. **The throttled-iterator row contradicts the rest of the design (903-907).**
   - It says the iterator retries "ANY error … exactly; kept".
   - Sections 1 and 6 require capability errors and `ClusterUnsplittableError` to be terminal in that same iterator (268-270, 1627-1628).
   - The code retries everything except `RunnerClosedError` (throttled_retrying_iterator.go:86-89). State the Go-only exceptions in the row.
4. **Redefining the arms silently weakens existing tests (1120-1124).**
   - `FaultConflict` and `FaultTransactionTooOld` are documented as a deliberate double-commit superset test (fault.go:24-34).
   - `TestSPFreshChaos_WritePathFaults:125-129` targets "the path most exposed to a non-idempotent replay". Under v14 it stays green but no longer replays a landed commit, and the same applies to the `FaultsAll` presets.
   - Needed: audit these tests and move the replay cases to `FaultCommitUnknown`.
5. **Pages: the design contradicts itself and leaves a reachable path without a fixture.**
   - 1131 says "A page is read-only, so 1020 and 1021 are not page outcomes". 1081-1084 says pages can write.
   - cascades_generator.go:2162-2171 says a first-page `not_committed` "is REACHED".
   - The fixture was narrowed to 1007 (1167-1168), which drops the reached path.
6. **The uncounted split-window retry is underspecified (977-992, 1093-1100).**
   - It does not say whether an uncounted retry waits the delay; without one it busy-loops.
   - "As today on every backend" is false for SimFDB, which caps at 100 today (1089-1092). Under D-0 each attempt sets limit 0, so the retry becomes unbounded.
   - In single-goroutine DST, a stalled seal whose takeover needs another actor to run would then hang.

## P3

- **The SimClock wait (998-999) breaks the clock's contract.**
  - `Clock` has only `Now()`, and `Advance` is documented as driver-only (pkg/dst/clock.go:18-33, 41-45).
