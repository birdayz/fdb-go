# RFC-282: Enqueue-only connection writer, configurable linger, and client hot-path optimizations

**Status:** Implemented
**Reference:** FoundationDB C++ 7.3.77 (`/tmp/fdbsrc`).
**Related:** RFC-259 (GRV batching), RFC-264 (send-wait cost), RFC-269 (monitor ping ownership), PR #991 (single-network-goroutine & D-NG lab).

## Problem

In `pprof` captures (`3,600,000` post-warmup `get100` transactions at 256 workers, `GOMAXPROCS=8`, `go1.27.3 linux/amd64` on a 16-vCPU AMD EPYC-Milan `ccx43` host; PR #991 commit `35705b26a`, `docs/labs/single-network-goroutine/rawhz7/prof/`), the pure-Go client at merge-base `e14dd5bea` spends 35–40% of its CPU and 55% of its heap allocations on internal synchronization and allocation overhead that has no counterpart in `libfdb_c`:

1. **Write-acknowledgment handoff (`pkg/fdbgo/transport`):** Callers wait for `writeLoop` (or a sender-turn / direct write) to confirm the frame reached the socket before waiting for the reply. In C++, `Peer::send` (`fdbrpc/FlowTransport.actor.cpp:1038-1043`) appends the packet to `unsent` and triggers `dataToSend` only when `firstUnsent` is true; `RequestStream::getReply` (`fdbrpc/include/fdbrpc/fdbrpc.h:742`) returns a `Future<Reply>` immediately, and `connectionWriter` (`fdbrpc/FlowTransport.actor.cpp:704-738`) drains `unsent` asynchronously. Waiting for a write acknowledgment adds a second wakeup per RPC (~9% of client CPU, `0.30` vs `0.06` futex/op).
2. **Rigid timer-based coalescing vs. configurable immediate/adaptive/fixed linger:** C++ `connectionWriter` waits `max(MIN_COALESCE_DELAY, MAX_COALESCE_DELAY - (now() - lastWriteTime))` (`10/20 µs`, `fdbrpc/FlowTransport.actor.cpp:708-710`, `flow/Knobs.cpp:211-212`; `BUGGIFY` sets both to `0`, confirming zero delay is wire-valid). In C++, actors share one network thread (`Net2::run`, `flow/Net2.actor.cpp:1506`), so frames accumulate while the thread runs other actors. In Go, callers run in parallel across `P`s; a default `10/20 µs` `hrtimer` sleep on an idle connection adds latency and a timer wakeup on every hop, whereas under `naWriter` (`D-NG`) frames coalesce naturally while `naLoop` is scheduled or inside `conn.Write`. While Go already exposes `WithCoalesceDelay(min, max)` across `transport`, `client`, and `fdb`, it defaults to `10/20 µs` timer waits and lacks a Nagle-style adaptive linger mode (`WithAdaptiveLinger(window)`) that flushes idle connections immediately (`sinceLastWrite >= window`) and yields (`runtime.Gosched()`, zero timer syscalls) only during active bursts (`sinceLastWrite < window`).
3. **Unsharded connection reply map (`Conn.pendingMu`):** A single `sync.RWMutex` guards `c.pending` across `PrepareReplyTo`, `Cancel`, and `readLoop`, accounting for **76.7% of all client mutex contention** (`10.65 s` cumulative delay over 3.6M `get100/256` ops), compounded by `readLoop` calling `time.Now()` inside the lock.
4. **Per-transaction timeout node, weak-pointer, and GC-cleanup churn:** `ReadYourWritesTransaction::resetTimeout` (`fdbclient/ReadYourWrites.actor.cpp:1567-1578`) runs one `timebomb` actor when `TIMEOUT > 0` (default `0` = `Void()`). On `master` (`pkg/fdbgo/client/read_incarnation.go:215-225`, `timeout_queue.go:97-118,228-254`), `armTimeoutLocked` calls `q.newNode(tx)` on every new `Transaction`, allocating `&timeoutNode{tx: weak.Make(tx)}`, registering `runtime.AddCleanup(tx, ...)` to remove the node on GC, and locking one of 16 shared `timeoutQueue` shards (`q.mu`) to push/sift the node into `q.heap`. Because `disarmTimeoutLocked` (`read_incarnation.go:233-235`) only clears `tx.timeoutArmed = false`, each completed transaction's `timeoutNode` stays in `q.heap` until either GC runs its cleanup callback (acquiring `q.mu` again to remove it) or the queue drainer reaches its next physical wake (`min(deadline + timeoutSlack, now + 10s)`). Under `Transact` with `-timeout-ms=5000`, `weak.Make(tx)`, `runtime.AddCleanup`, `&timeoutNode` allocation, and shared-heap locking consume **4.4% of total client CPU (`5.71 s`)** and 2 allocs/tx even when transactions finish in ~1 ms.
5. **Hot-path allocations and lock contention in `client`:**
   - `GetPipelined` allocates a `map[*PendingGet]struct{}` (`tx.pendingReads`) even for single-read transactions, eagerly acquires a `5 s` `time.Timer` before `PendingGet.resolve` checks if the reply is already ready, and `snapshotCache.insertPoint` allocates map/slice headers on a single-key read.
   - `latencySketch.addSample` (`pkg/fdbgo/client/ddsketch.go`, port of `DDSketch.h:86`) computes `math.Log` under a single database-wide `sync.Mutex` (**11.4% of mutex contention**).
   - `cachedOrFetchReadVersion` (`pkg/fdbgo/client/read_version_state.go`) allocates `&pendingReadVersion`, `make(chan struct{})`, and `make(chan grvResult, 1)` on every transaction (3 allocs/tx) even when only one goroutine awaits the transaction's GRV.
   - `Transact`'s final commit runs `startTurnover().finish()`, allocating a successor `readIncarnation`, span, and `versionstampCompletion` on a `Transaction` that is discarded immediately after `Transact` returns (9 allocs/tx).

## Proposed Change

### 1. Enqueue-only per-connection writer (`D-NG`) and configurable linger (`pkg/fdbgo/transport`)
- **Enqueue-only send (`T1`, `T2`):** `naWriter` maintains a double-buffered contiguous byte slice (`w.q` ↔ `spare`, pre-sized to 64 KiB) guarded by `w.mu`. Small frames (≤ 4 KiB) are stack-encoded (`var stackBuf [4128]byte`) before acquiring `w.mu` so `w.mu` holds only a `copy` and slice advance, with zero `writeFramePool` traffic. `w.sig` (`chan struct{}`, cap 1) is signalled only on an `empty → non-empty` transition (`len(w.q) == 0`, matching `firstUnsent` in `Peer::send`, `FlowTransport.actor.cpp:1042-1043`). On `D-NG`, `SendFrame`, `SendFrameContext`, `SendFrameDeferred`, `SendFrameDeferredContext`, and `SendFrameEagerContext` return once the frame is appended to `w.q`, while `Flush` and `FlushContext` validate `ctx`, cold-dial admission, and connection liveness (`c.ctx.Done()`) without waiting for `naLoop`'s asynchronous socket write.
- **Bounded high-water backpressure:** To bound memory if a peer stalls its TCP receive window before `connectionMonitor` (`2 s`) closes the connection, `naSend` blocks when `len(w.q) >= 16 MiB` (`naHighWater`) until `naLoop` drains `w.q`, `ctx.Done()`, or `c.ctx.Done()`. Once a frame is appended to `w.q`, cancelling the caller's `ctx` ends only the caller's reply wait and never un-queues or truncates a frame (`FlowTransport.actor.cpp:1038-1043`).
- **Write errors:** `naLoop` writes `buf` in full; any `conn.Write` error calls `c.failConnection(codedConnClosed(err))`, failing all pending replies with FDB code `1030` (`request_maybe_delivered`, `fdbrpc.h:794-799`).
- **Default linger change and configurable linger (`WithCoalesceDelay` and new `WithAdaptiveLinger`):**
  - **Default behavior change:** Under the `D-NG` (`naWriter`) default writer, connections default to immediate flush on writer wakeup (`minCoalesce = 0, maxCoalesce = 0, adaptiveLinger = 0, waits = false`), coalescing frames that arrive while `naLoop` is scheduled or inside `conn.Write` without an `hrtimer` sleep. `DefaultMinCoalesceDelay` (`10 µs`) and `DefaultMaxCoalesceDelay` (`20 µs`) remain exported constants for `libfdb_c`'s `MIN/MAX_COALESCE_DELAY` (and remain the default when `WithLegacyWriter()` is selected). Option doc comments on `transport.WithCoalesceDelay`, `client.WithCoalesceDelay`, and `fdb.WithCoalesceDelay` and `DIVERGENCES.md` / `CHANGELOG.md` are updated to document the `0/0` `D-NG` default.
  - Existing `WithCoalesceDelay(minDelay, maxDelay)` (`transport.DialOption`, `client.Option`, `fdb.Option`) is wired into `naWriter`: it sets `minCoalesce, maxCoalesce = max(minDelay, 0), max(maxDelay, 0)` and clears `adaptiveLinger`. When `min > 0 || max > 0`, `naLoop` enables `w.delay = true`, creates `c.coalesce` (`hrtimer` on Linux), and waits `coalesceWait(time.Since(c.lastWrite))` (`max(min, max - sinceLastWrite)` jittered `0.9–1.1`, `FlowTransport.actor.cpp:708-710`) before draining `w.q`. When `0, 0`, `w.delay = false` and `naLoop` flushes immediately on wakeup.
  - New `WithAdaptiveLinger(window time.Duration)` (`transport.DialOption`, `client.Option`, `fdb.Option`, and `tools/clientbench -adaptive-linger`): configures Nagle-style adaptive linger (`adaptiveLinger = max(window, 0)`, clearing `minCoalesce, maxCoalesce` and `w.delay`). If `time.Since(c.lastWrite) >= window` (idle connection), `naLoop` flushes immediately; if `time.Since(c.lastWrite) < window` (active burst), `naLoop` yields (`runtime.Gosched()`, zero timer syscalls) up to `c.lastWrite.Add(window)` before draining `w.q`.
  - The last option applied wins; `Conn.CoalesceDelay()` and new `Conn.AdaptiveLinger()` report the active runtime configuration. `WithLegacyWriter()` retains the legacy `writeLoop` for unit tests of legacy writer-turn/write-ack internals.

### 2. 32-shard `Conn.pending` reply table & per-connection reply tokens (`pkg/fdbgo/transport`)
- Replace `Conn.pendingMu` + `Conn.pending` with 32 cache-line-padded shards (`[32]pendingShard`, `sync.Mutex` + `map[UID]pendingEntry`) indexed by `(token.First ^ (token.First >> 17)) & 31`.
- In `readLoop`, sample `recvAt := time.Now()` before acquiring `shard.mu.Lock()`.
- Mint reply tokens from a per-connection atomic counter (`c.nextReplyToken()`) seeded with `splitmix64()`, avoiding global contention while keeping reply tokens unique per connection.

### 3. Pooled `Transact`-scoped timeout timers (`pkg/fdbgo/client`)
- For `Transaction`s with `TIMEOUT` set (`ReadYourWrites.actor.cpp:1567-1578`) on a production `timeoutQueue` (no test clock/hooks installed), `designArmLocked` bypasses `q.newNode(tx)` (`weak.Make(tx)` + `runtime.AddCleanup` + `&timeoutNode` + `q.heap` push) and uses one `designTimerEntry` per transaction.
- **Synchronization and stale-fire protection across pooled reuse:**
  - Each `designTimerEntry` has its own `e.mu sync.Mutex` guarding `e.tx *Transaction`, `e.arm uint64`, `e.deadline int64`, `e.nextFire int64`, and `e.t *time.Timer`.
  - When `Transact` / `ReadTransact` arms a transaction (`tx.transactScoped = true`), under `tx.readErrMu` and `e.mu` it sets `e.tx = tx`, `e.arm = tx.timeoutArm`, and `e.deadline = now + int64(delay)`. If `e.t == nil`, it creates `time.AfterFunc(delay, e.fire)`; if `e.nextFire <= now || deadline < e.nextFire`, it updates `e.nextFire = deadline` and calls `e.t.Reset(delay)`; otherwise (`now < e.nextFire <= deadline`), it skips `e.t.Reset` and leaves `e.t` scheduled for the earlier `e.nextFire`.
  - When `e.t` fires (`e.fire()`), it locks `e.mu`: if `e.tx == nil` (entry is disarmed or back in `designTimerPool`), it clears `e.nextFire = 0`, unlocks `e.mu`, and returns. If `e.tx != nil` and `rem := e.deadline - monoNow(); rem > 0` (i.e., an earlier owner's `nextFire` fired before the current owner's `deadline`, mimicking C++ `timebomb`'s `while (now() < endTime)` loop at `ReadYourWrites.actor.cpp:1571`), `e.fire()` sets `e.nextFire = e.deadline`, calls `e.t.Reset(time.Duration(rem))`, unlocks `e.mu`, and returns without timing out the transaction. Only when `monoNow() >= e.deadline` does `e.fire()` clear `e.nextFire = 0`, unlock `e.mu`, and call `tx.fireTimeout(arm)`, which re-verifies `tx.timeoutArmed && tx.timeoutArm == arm` under `tx.readErrMu`.
  - On `Transact` / `ReadTransact` exit (`releaseTransactTimeout`) or `disarmTimeoutLocked` (`stopDesignTimerLocked`), the caller locks `e.mu` and clears `e.tx = nil` before returning `e` to `designTimerPool`, preventing any concurrent `e.fire()` from observing the released transaction. Non-`Transact` transactions (or transactions that leave `Transact` with in-flight reads) use a per-transaction weak `time.AfterFunc` (`tx.designTimer` with `weak.Make(tx)`) so unreferenced armed transactions remain GC-collectable; `fireDesignTimeout` checks `tx.designDeadline - monoNow() > 0` under `tx.readErrMu` and re-arms `tx.designTimer` if an in-flight callback from an earlier expiration runs after `SetTimeout` or `Reset` extended the deadline. Tests that inject custom clocks or hooks continue through `timeoutQueue`.

### 4. Client hot-path allocation & contention reductions (`pkg/fdbgo/client`)
- **`GetPipelined` & `PendingGet`:** Add scalar `pendingReadFirst *PendingGet` on `Transaction` so single-read transactions avoid allocating `map[*PendingGet]struct{}`; embed `keyBuf [64]byte` in `PendingGet`; defer `getTimer(readRPCTimeout)` in `PendingGet.resolve` until after a non-blocking `select` on `p.replyCh`, using Go 1.23+ single-call `Timer.Reset`; add inline single-entry fast path to `snapshotCache.insertPoint`.
- **16-stripe `latencySketch`:** Compute `bucketIndex(v)` (`math.Log`) before locking and stripe `latencySketch` across 16 cache-line-padded shards merged in `stats()`.
- **Inline `pendingReadVersion` & pooled `grvReplyPool`:** Embed `inlinePending` on `readVersionState` with `done chan struct{}` allocated lazily under `readVersionMu` only when a second concurrent waiter joins; pool `chan grvResult` (cap 1) in `grvBatcher.getReadVersion` on the non-cancelled path.
- **Final-commit turnover skip in `Transact` & inline `versionstampCompletion`:** Embed `inlineVs versionstampCompletion` on `Transaction` so the first commit of any transaction avoids heap-allocating `&versionstampCompletion`, and set `tx.transactFinalCommit = true` inside `Database.Transact` so a successful final `Commit` skips eagerly allocating the unused post-commit successor `readIncarnation` and trace span (regenerating the span and arming any remaining timeout lazily if the handle is reused after `Transact`). Move `shipReadConflicts` after the read-only fast path in `commitAdmitted` (`NativeAPI.actor.cpp:6800-6806`).

## Wire-Compatibility Impact

- **Design invariant:** Frame encoding (`encodeFrameInto`), conflict ranges, error codes (`1030` `request_maybe_delivered` on socket write error), and logical request sets are unchanged; what changes is (1) the client-internal handoff between sender goroutines and the per-connection writer goroutine (`naSend` → `naLoop`), and (2) the default and configurable flush linger (`0/0` immediate default on `D-NG`, `WithCoalesceDelay(min, max)` fixed timer coalescing, and `WithAdaptiveLinger(window)` Nagle-style adaptive linger).
- **Verification gates (implemented and run in this PR):**
  - Socket-level frame integrity, ordering, cancellation invariants (`T1`/`T2`), high-water backpressure, and write-error propagation (`1030`) after `naSend → naLoop → conn.Write` are verified by unit and concurrency tests in `pkg/fdbgo/transport:transport_test` (`design_writer_test.go`, `conn_coalesce_test.go`) reading raw frames from the peer `net.Conn`.
  - Client-layer logical request stability (Steps 3–7) is checked by `tools/clientbench/wiregolden:wiregolden_test`, which captures requests in-process via `transport.CaptureWireLog` (`LogSend`, before the writer queue), compares the normalized Go request transcript against `go.golden`, and asserts the documented diff from `libfdbc.golden` (`go-divergences.diff`).
  - In the PR #991 prototype lab (`commit c645f6b47`), a 1-worker socket `tcpdump` capture observed `1.00` GRV/tx, `2.004` proxy frames/tx, and `847.6 B`/tx on the wire. Packet coalescing boundaries on the socket depend on the configured linger mode (`WithCoalesceDelay` / `WithAdaptiveLinger`), matching `flow/Knobs.cpp:211-212` where `MIN/MAX_COALESCE_DELAY` is a transport performance knob tested at `0` under `BUGGIFY`.

## Executable Spec & Test Plan

- `pkg/fdbgo/transport:transport_test` (`design_writer_test.go`, `conn_coalesce_test.go`, `conn_coalesce_linux_test.go`):
  - `TestDesignSendIsEnqueueOnly`: proves `T1` across `0` linger, fixed `min,max` delay, and `WithAdaptiveLinger` using a writer-gated socket (`0` socket writes completed before `SendFrameContext` returns).
  - `TestDesignAdaptiveLingerBurstAndIdle`: proves `WithAdaptiveLinger` flushes immediately when idle (`sinceLastWrite >= window`), coalesces active bursts (`sinceLastWrite < window`) into one socket write, and exits promptly on `Close`.
  - `TestDesignCancelNeverUnqueues`: proves `T2` (`ctx` cancel after send never drops or alters queued frames; `ctx` already cancelled before send queues nothing).
  - `TestDesignWriteErrorFailsAllPending`: proves a socket write error fails all pending replies with `ErrConnClosed` / code `1030`.
  - `TestDesignConcurrentSendersFramesWholeOnce`: 16 concurrent senders × 50 frames (including `<= 4 KiB` stack-encoded and `> 4 KiB` pooled frames) arrive whole and in per-sender order.
  - `TestCoalesceDelayOption` & `TestDNGCoalesceDelaySharesOneWriteAndEndsOnClose`: verifies `D-NG`'s `0/0` default, `WithCoalesceDelay(min, max)`, `WithCoalesceDelay(0, 0)`, and `WithAdaptiveLinger(d)` last-wins precedence, legacy-writer fallback, and `CoalesceDelay()` / `AdaptiveLinger()` reporting.
- `pkg/fdbgo/client`:
  - `timeout_design_unit_test.go`: verifies `D4` timeout delivery (`1031`), disarm and stale-callback rejection across `SetTimeout` and `Reset`, `Database.Transact` / `Database.ReadTransact` pooled `designTimerEntry` selection and release, and pooled `designTimerEntry` reuse where an earlier transaction's timer fires while a second transaction with a later deadline owns the entry (re-arming for the remaining budget without delivering a false `1031`).
  - `coalesce_option_test.go`, `ddsketch_test.go`, and all 35 `transport` + `client` + `fdb` targets under `-race`.
